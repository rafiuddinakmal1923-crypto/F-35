## How to read this

- Voltages are generated by the load-flow model, because the open datasets hold kWh only. These results show the voltage locator works on physics-consistent data, not that it works on TNB meters (SRS AS-3).
- Flag threshold: 5% of supply plus the uncertainty margin, for 7 days. A single partial bypass is usually a few percent of a zone's supply, so on its own it often stays below this threshold; such thefts are counted as missed above, not hidden.
- Weights: tuned on the tuning split only (24 zones, seed 1000); never tuned on held-out or stress settings.
- Pattern model training data: sgcc. When the SGCC download is not available it is trained on synthetic windows that follow the SGCC theft patterns; its AUC then measures fit to those patterns, not real-world performance.
- 'Ranked zones' are the zones the pipeline ranks: flagged zones and zones without a transformer meter.
