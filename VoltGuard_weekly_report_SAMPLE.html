Assumptions and limits

  • Simulated data. This prototype runs on a simulated region built from public household profiles; voltages come from a network model, because open data hold energy readings only.
  • Technical loss is estimated by an engineering model of cables and transformers; its error is part of the margin of error.
  • Monthly-read meters are spread across the month using similar homes' daily patterns; this also widens the margin.
  • Small leaks can hide: a tap of about 1% of a zone's supply sits inside the margin and will not flag the zone.
  • Scores rank risk; they are not findings of theft. People decide after inspection.
  • Tariff not yet supplied, so value at risk is shown in kWh only.
  • Zone-level only: this report holds no premise IDs, names or addresses, and covers region CEN only.
