A VoltGuard case report has these sections:

1. Case summary: case ID, premise ID, zone, dates of detection, inspection and outcome.
2. Account holder and premise address: filled in by the case service only when the approved report
   is exported. Drafts hold the placeholders {{HOLDER_NAME}}, {{ADDRESS}} and {{CONTACT}}.
3. Zone evidence: energy balance, gap start, size of the unexplained energy and its hourly profile.
4. Premise evidence: the risk score's main reasons, voltage evidence and usage against similar homes.
5. Inspection findings: the redacted inspector notes and the number of photos.
6. Relevant legal provisions, cited from the approved public texts.
7. Approvals: the analyst approves the content; a manager, a different person, approves the export.

Wording describes risk and evidence. It never states that a person is a thief; that is for the
investigating authority to decide.
