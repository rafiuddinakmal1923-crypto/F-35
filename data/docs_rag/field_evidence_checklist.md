This is a team-authored draft for the prototype. It is not a TNB procedure; utility procedures
replace it in R1.

## Before the visit

- Check whether the case is marked as a joint operation. For a large or flat 24/7 hidden load, wait
  for the police and the joint-operation team. [TNB procedure: to be supplied]
- Carry the case on the inspector app; the address appears only on cases assigned to your team.

## At the meter

- Photograph the meter board, the meter seal and the incoming cables before touching anything.
- Look for cables that bypass the meter, broken or replaced seals, and loads that continue when the
  meter shows little or no use.
- Do not cut, move or disconnect cables yourself. Record what you see and photograph it.
- Evidence handling and seal procedure: [TNB procedure: to be supplied]

## Recording

- Photos taken in the app stay inside the app; they are not saved to the phone's camera roll.
- Choose the outcome that matches what you found. If you could not enter, record No access.
- Keep notes factual: what was seen, where, and the measured load if known.
